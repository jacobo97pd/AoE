## Ownership

| Layer | Owns | Must not own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simulation | Entities, rule state, validated commands, movement, eras/research, factions, effective unit stats, fixed ticks | GameObjects, camera, rendering, Unity input |
| Presentation | Primitive models, team readability, camera, selection display, HUD | Authoritative positions, resources, costs or victory rules |
| Input adapter | Pointer/touch gestures and player intentions | Direct mutation of rule state |
| Composition | Definition loading, world creation, tick scheduling, adapter wiring | A hidden global service locator |
| Editor tools | Repeatable scene setup, data validation, build entry points | Runtime gameplay rules |
| Diagnostics | Versioned stress fixtures, independent observation, optional timing and reports | Timing-dependent game decisions |

Simulation is a plain C# assembly with `noEngineReferences`. Stable integer entity IDs cross the boundary; presentation maps them to objects. Pure serializable definitions use stable string IDs and explicit validation. Runtime objects are not balance databases. ScriptableObject authoring can wrap plain definitions later without creating a simulation dependency on Unity.

## Initial implementation choices

- Authoritative positions use integer millimetres; render positions convert to Unity metres. Simulate at **20 ticks per second** through a central driver, and keep display smoothing outside the rules.
- Submit commands through a validation boundary. Four-neighbour static routing, shared reverse route fields, reachable combat firing-position searches and separate formation planning feed radius-aware local movement. Continuous swept-body/static checks prevent tunneling and body penetration; bounded local recovery handles ordinary congestion.
- Use a small handcrafted map and primitive units/buildings/resource nodes. Shared materials keep team and role silhouettes readable.
- Camera controls support touch pan/pinch and mouse equivalents, with map limits and no rotation. Selection owns local UI state; accepted orders own simulation changes.
- Keep touch gestures separate from HUD hit handling. The mobile input scene should expose selection, orders and camera checks without requiring keyboard shortcuts.

## Growth path

The current world composes **movement, economy, construction, combat, faction actions, production and research** in that fixed order, then increments `TickIndex`. Faction influence and carry/speed state refresh before and after movement, after faction timers/transitions and after research. Harvesting therefore sees the worker's current coverage; combat destruction prevents same-tick charter completion or outpost conversion; a finished Muster charter can affect production immediately. Research effects still become available to attacks and harvesting on the following tick. Input and HUD adapters submit commands for inventories, construction, training, research and faction actions; they do not mutate those authoritative collections or timers directly.

Manual delivery preserves an existing worker load across stop/reassignment and lets it reach a completed owned drop-off even when no gatherable sources remain. Building placement blocks its footprint immediately and replans movement; completed housing grants capacity once. Training charges and reserves population when queued, then waits without a duplicate charge if its spawn area is blocked. New foundations and produced units receive presentation objects when the view synchronizes. Detailed invariants and current limits are in `SIMULATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

Phase 3 adds health/armor, tagged soft counters, explicit attacks, automatic target acquisition, chasing, ranged projectiles and entity death. Acquisition runs at 10-tick intervals and chase routing at 5-tick intervals. Existing projectile impacts resolve before living actors choose attacks; staged melee hits permit mutual kills. Homing projectiles have a configurable 400-tick default lifetime and no terrain occlusion or line-of-sight checks. Highest matching tag bonuses apply once before armor, with a minimum-one successful hit.

Unit death releases population and loses cargo. Destroyed buildings cancel queues without refunds, release reservations and completed housing capacity, and clear their navigation footprints. Existing units remain if housing loss leaves the player over capacity; production is constrained until capacity recovers. Construction progress grants health incrementally so completion does not heal combat damage. Repair, voluntary queue cancellation/refunds, building attacks and full victory conditions remain outside this slice.

Phase 4 separates destination assignment (`FormationPlanner`) from routes (`Navigation`) and local motion (`MovementSystem`). Loose, Line and Box plans adapt to available space. A reusable spatial grid supports swept movement checks; cached segments limit redundant visibility work. Shared fields retain at most four entries and invalidate on connectivity changes. Local recovery is limited to four searches per tick, a 625-node lattice per search and a per-unit cooldown. These local bounds are not an overall command/path-work budget. See [pathfinding architecture](PATHFINDING_ARCHITECTURE.md) for exact policies.

The historical Phase 4 stress scenarios exercised 50–500 movers independently of armed combat. All tested open, wide-corridor, crossing and dynamic-foundation cases completed, while the one-cell choke remained congested at 200+. Cold command batches can exceed a display-frame budget. Fog, AI and full match flow follow the roadmap. The Phase 4 military archetypes passed the 12 mirrored nominal-value counter scenarios; those bounded outcomes do not establish broad competitive balance or verify faction effects.

## Phase 5 progression and research

`TechnologyCatalog` validates plain `EraDefinition`, `TechnologyDefinition` and `TechnologyEffect` data before a world starts. Configured eras must form all four unique tiers with exactly one adjacent advancement for each tier after the first. Technology IDs and references must be valid and unique where required; direct prerequisite cycles, inaccessible producers and later-era self-locks reject the configuration. A fixed-point reachability pass checks building unlocks and technology prerequisites from tier 1 through the complete era chain. Costs must be nonnegative, research duration positive, and cumulative matching attack/armor/gather additions must fit `int`. Empty or null era/technology arrays preserve legacy fixtures; no-era players expose tier 1 and a null era ID. Authored sandbox spawns bypass era/technology unlock gates but retain faction validation and do not select a later starting era.

`ResearchSystem` accepts `ResearchCommand` only at a completed owned producer of the required definition. It checks the player's minimum era, completed technologies and actual completed owned prerequisite buildings. Era advancement instead requires the exact preceding current tier and excludes concurrent era advancement. One technology cannot run twice for the same player. Research and training are mutually exclusive within one producer, while separate buildings may research different technologies. The complete cost is paid once at acceptance. Unit training and new building placement also enforce their definition's era and technology gates.

Prerequisites are checked when research begins. Losing another required building afterward does not cancel accepted work. Destroying the producer before its completion step loses the spent cost and progress, clears pending research identity and permits a retry at full cost; completed technologies persist. No cancellation/refund UI or command is included. Combat runs before production and research, so destruction wins on a would-be completion tick.

Completion recomputes matching owned-unit `AttackDamage`, `Armor` and `GatherAmount` from base definitions into a per-player cache. Existing units update once, future spawns receive cached values, and a unit spawned earlier in the same completion tick is included. Definitions and enemy units remain unchanged. An effect matches any overlapping target tag once. Damage uses upgraded attack before the highest existing tag multiplier and target armor, retaining minimum-one damage for an armed attacker; an effective zero-damage unit remains unarmed. Already launched projectiles preserve their final damage snapshot. Gather upgrades retain cargo and node-stock limits. Combat and harvesting have already run on the completion tick, so upgraded actions begin on the following tick.

The HUD reads player era/completed technologies, producer `ActiveResearch` progress and current unit stats. `World.ValidateResearch` and `ValidateRequirements` provide read-only legality checks and display-name prerequisite feedback. UI selection and progress rendering stay outside simulation; buttons submit commands. Detailed contracts and exclusions are in [simulation architecture](SIMULATION_ARCHITECTURE.md), with authored content described in the [technology tree](../design/TECHNOLOGY_TREE.md) and [building roster](../design/BUILDING_ROSTER.md).

## Phase 6 factions and relocation

`FactionCatalog` validates `GameDefinition.Factions` and copies optional `MapDefinition.PlayerFactions` entries into immutable player assignments. Omitted arrays leave old maps neutral. Unit/building/technology `RequiredFactionId` gates reject wrong-faction and neutral purchases before any payment. Authored faction-specific spawns require the actual matching assignment, even though authored era/technology gates remain bypassed for sandbox setup. The shared Storeyard's charter capability does not faction-lock the building itself. Assignments cannot change during a world; a menu choice creates a new world.

Configuration checks include supported kinds and action roles, references, positive bounded durations/radii, nonnegative costs/bonuses, matching-stat overflow and per-faction progression closure. Neutral and each faction must reach their applicable content without borrowing another faction's infrastructure or technologies. The catalog does not prove that a particular map has resources or space for a build order. The transport definition must be unarmed, nonworker, population-free, unavailable for direct training and equal in maximum health to its unique originating outpost. Relocatable structures cannot supply housing. The [faction bible](../design/FACTION_BIBLE.md) records numeric tuning and the limits of the asymmetric roster.

`FactionSystem` handles `SetCharterCommand`, `DeployThreadkeeperCommand`, `PackOutpostCommand`, `DeployOutpostCommand` and `RepositionCommand`. Read-only `ValidateFactionAction`/`ValidateFactionRequirement` support presentation feedback. The same transaction discipline used by research applies: every selected entity, ownership condition, action state, footprint and resource check completes before mutation. Active/cooldown timers, effective carry/speed, charter source IDs, producer work rate and relocation stages are simulation state; markers and selected-object details are presentation state.

Aven workers gain +2 carry. Storeyard charter initialization or switching costs 40 Wood/20 Metal and takes 100 ticks. Logistics grants +4 local carry; Muster grants +200 permille nearby training work, with a per-entry integer remainder instead of rounded extra work every tick. Overlapping sources do not stack; the lowest source yard ID resolves equivalent support. A 160-work-tick recruit requires 134 uninterrupted ticks at this work rate. Charters are exclusive per yard, their 6 m coverage is center-to-center, and switches suppress all old charter benefit while ordinary drop-off remains usable. Research/training and a charter change exclude one another on the producer. Losing a carry bonus never discards existing cargo; over-capacity loads can still be physically delivered.

A Threadkeeper immediately commits to 300 immobile ticks, then a 400-tick cooldown, when deployed within an active owned Storeyard's tether radius. It relays the source charter within 4 m and is neither a resource generator nor a drop-off. There are no relay chains. Move and Stop reject while deployed. Lost tether or a switching source supplies no benefit; switching leaves the deployment timer running. Source destruction ends deployment and starts full cooldown. Reciprocal Stores expands yard coverage/tether to 8 m after completion, retaining a 4 m relay. Threadkeepers and packed outposts remain unarmed even under custom attack effects.

Packing a completed Supply Outpost disables `IsOperational` immediately, preventing deposits, training and research and causing affected carriers to seek another drop-off. Nonempty production or active research prevents packing. After 100 ticks, the stationary footprint clears and the same ID becomes a vulnerable cart at the same center. Its normal movement uses existing collision/pathfinding. Exact damage survives both conversions; neither conversion increments `DeathCount`, changes population/reservations, charges resources or refunds the original building. Actual death removes the asset once. Presentation must detect both entity-kind and definition changes under a stable ID when replacing its view.

Deployment accepts only a stopped cart adjacent to an aligned, legal footprint within the normal half-cell interaction distance. It takes 100 ticks and reserves no navigation footprint while pending. Completion revalidates static cells and unit-circle clearance with tangency legal; obstruction leaves an immobile vulnerable cart at zero remaining ticks until space clears. These retry checks use direct geometry rather than copying/rebuilding the entire navigation grid. Stop cancels deployment progress, returning the same unhealed cart to Packed; Move is then available. Successful deployment restores a completed building, blocks the new adjacent footprint and replans routes. Prepared Encampments reduces each transition by 40 ticks. Authoritative `RelocationTotalTicks` captures the accepted duration alongside `RelocationRemainingTicks`, so research completed mid-action cannot change its progress denominator; cancellation and newly converted states reset the total to zero. Validation keeps each reduced duration positive. Relocation offers no remote building placement, free rebuild or terrain bypass.

Serevin cavalry gains +50 permille speed. Ashrunner reposition adds +500 permille for 60 ticks, followed by a 300-tick cooldown starting at expiry: base 5000 mm/s becomes 5250 normally and 7750 while active. It cancels prior combat and suppresses explicit/automatic attacks throughout the burst, while Move/Stop, collisions and incoming damage still work. Stop holds fire after expiry. Existing cavalry tags preserve spear counterplay; the action grants no invulnerability or damage addition.

Faction state buffers are reused and neutral worlds skip local influence traversal. Attack/armor/gather research modifiers remain completion-time caches; carry/speed reflect changing positions and active actions. Live influence and deployment checks still scan their relevant sources/bodies. These costs, new HUD work and faction-scale behavior have not been profiled; fixed housing rules and cache reuse do not establish a frame budget or mobile performance. Do not adopt ECS, Jobs or Burst before profiling identifies a suitable bottleneck. The plain simulation boundary permits future batching but does not guarantee a cost-free networking or ECS migration.
