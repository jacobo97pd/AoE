# Emberfield native art slice provenance

The Phase 9 Aven kit is original procedural geometry written for this workspace. It uses no downloaded meshes, textures, portraits, traced reference images, generated bitmap assets or third-party model packs. The supplied RTS reference informed broad readability and production constraints; it supplied no copied artwork or faction symbols.

Editable recipes live in [ArtKitGeometry.cs](../../Assets/Game/Presentation/ArtKitGeometry.cs). The [editor baker](../../Assets/Game/Editor/ArtKitBaker.cs) creates ordinary editable Unity mesh assets, a catalog and one shared material. Run **Emberfield → Art → Bake original Aven slice**, or invoke `Emberfield.Editor.ArtKitBaker.Bake` in the existing editor. There is no automatic import-time asset rewrite. Rebaking preserves asset identities.

The authored set contains Tender, Reedguard, Stringwarden and Strider; Hearth, Shelter and Muster Hall; and Food, Wood, Metal and Stone. Aven forms use angular tunics, square framing, stepped civic roofs and broad cloth panels. Role distinctions come from the pack/tool mass, tall spear, angular bow and long mounted profile. Resources use a harvest plot, faceted tree crown, amber-bearing stone and pale rock clusters. Faction shape remains independent of the owner's team tint.

The final recipes include team cloth across both roof slopes and a Tender pack wrap/rear band at both LODs, added after actual match captures showed that the fixed camera hid front-only markings. Presentation turns building facades 180° around Y toward that camera. These are original geometry/presentation revisions and preserve the authored simulation footprints.

Each entry has two merged, flat-shaded mesh levels and one renderer per active level. Vertex RGB stores the shared material palette; vertex alpha marks team-colored cloth. One original [URP shader](../../Assets/Game/Resources/Shaders/FacetedTeam.shader) applies the owner's tint through a material property block, with no per-instance mesh or material clone. Its rendering support uses the installed Unity URP shader libraries. Geometry and palette recipes are the source of record; this is native mesh authoring, not a texture atlas or painted team-mask texture pipeline.

The baker writes [ART_KIT_MANIFEST.json](../../Assets/Game/Resources/Art/ART_KIT_MANIFEST.json) with the recipe SHA-256, every asset path, actual vertex/triangle counts, bounds and declared budgets. Per-entry triangle ceilings are 1,500/900 for units, 2,000/1,000 for buildings and 1,000/500 for resources at LOD0/LOD1. These modest forms deliberately use less geometry than the earlier art-bible planning ranges. They are not mobile frame-rate claims.

The successful final geometry bake produced 22 meshes using 2,388 LOD0 and 1,412 LOD1 triangles across one copy of all eleven identities. Recipe SHA-256: `e6c0ad5dd5451c847d4ed2a1a4fdd4a53bc991275a8e6cc04d85bcea7f5dbe5a`. This matches the editable source and includes the roof/pack revisions.

[AmberBiome.cs](../../Assets/Game/Presentation/AmberBiome.cs) supplies the original flat terrain mesh and sage/sand vertex palette, using local procedural variation and a pale route; [AmberSurface.shader](../../Assets/Game/Resources/Shaders/AmberSurface.shader) renders it through the installed Unity pipeline. Neither source consumes a landscape photograph, texture pack or external height map. Terrain colors do not grant movement or resource information.

[SliceFeedback.cs](../../Assets/Game/Presentation/SliceFeedback.cs) authors a small spark mesh and six deterministic audio cues from local oscillators, envelopes and seeded noise. The cues are mono at 22,050 Hz and use no sampled recordings, third-party music, voice performance or external audio-generation service. Spark geometry is shared by a 16-item effect pool; six reusable voices and cue throttles bound playback. Terrain and VFX use their own materials in addition to the kit's single shared mesh material. The sound toggle and visible-event filtering belong to presentation, without gameplay animation events.

Units face +Z with their feet at ground level. Mounted height is below 2.3 m, the Reedguard spear below 2.15 m, building height below 2.3 m, and resource width/depth below 1.4 m with the tree below 2.5 m. Building recipes stay inside a normalized 1×1 footprint, scaled to the simulation's authored width and depth. The kit returns `null` for unauthored entries so existing presentation can retain its fallback.

Validation status: PC engineering verified. Asset baking and shader compilation succeeded; both asset validity tests and the motion/feedback isolation test passed within the full **347 EditMode + 66 PlayMode** run. Packaged galleries at 1280×720 and 1440×1080 passed with 16 instances, 22 shared LOD meshes and one kit material; [the Phase 9 report](../tasks/PHASE_9_REPORT.md) links captures and smoke results. Cosmetic whole-model motion belongs to presentation; this kit provides no skeleton, skinning, motion capture or authored skeletal animation clips. Physical mobile validation remains unverified. The bounded slice does not establish a complete faction library, competitive readability or measured device performance.
